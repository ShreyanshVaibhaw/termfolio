    About the rankers

    Learned ranker
    Logistic regression trained on Inspec. It uses where a phrase appears, how often it occurs, and other candidate features.
    TF-IDF
    Rewards terms that recur in the source and are rare in the training abstracts.
    RAKE
    Rewards content words that appear together in phrases.

    Higher scores rank first within each reply. The methods use different score scales.
